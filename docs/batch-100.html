Missing, Mixed, or Zero?

A data-quality guide with an editable local example. No upload, external library, or service.

Work the denominator

Six rows × five fields = 30 cells. One revenue is missing: 29/30 = 96.67% completeness. That is not accuracy or trust. “pending” is present text; 0 is numeric and present.

Read the numeric subset

Revenue has five numeric values: mean 138, median 150, range −30 to 240. Orders has five numeric values and one text value: numeric mean 13.8. Report numeric counts alongside mixed-column means. The full app uses population standard deviation.

Compare scopes, not just numbers

Filter North: three rows, 100% completeness, revenue mean 80. South: three rows, 93.33% completeness, revenue mean 225 over only two numeric revenues. Search matches any cell by substring; reset before full-data comparisons.

Export evidence

Open current rows in Analytics; export summary JSON and filtered CSV. Visualize filtered data explicitly replaces the shared workspace; ordinary navigation does not apply a filter. No-match filters must not replace the workspace with empty data.
